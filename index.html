Touch Grass

A calming pop-up storybook in a meadow you can touch. Brush the grass with your hands. Pinch the glowing corner to turn the page and the world pops up from the book. Pinch the characters to help Pip find the moon.

On Quest: press Enter VR and put the controllers down.
On desktop: click to interact, hover the pen over the words to highlight them, scroll or use the arrow keys to look around.
